---
title: RESTORE Council Funded Trail Expansion Opens at Gulf State Park, Gulf
  Shores, AL
date: 2026-10-28
url: https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USRESTORECOUNCIL/bulletins/430c205
---
Alabama State Parks has officially completed a 4.4-mile expansion of the Fort Morgan Trail at Gulf State Park. The $5.6 million infrastructure upgrade was made possible through funding awarded by the Gulf Coast Ecosystem Restoration Council (RESTORE Council).
