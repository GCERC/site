On July 29, 2026, the Gulf Coast Ecosystem Restoration Council (Council) voted to approve
additional funding for the ongoing Pensacola Bay Living Shoreline Phase 1 implementation
project in Escambia County, Florida. Specifically, the Council voted to approve $748,010 in
additional funding to cover construction, engineering, and inspection costs associated with sand excavation and signage requirements for this living shoreline project. Background on this approval is provided below.
